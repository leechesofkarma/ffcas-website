# How to Update the Website

You don't need to know any code. Everything is done through simple forms.

## Logging in
1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**.
2. Sign in using the invite link that was emailed to you (or with GitHub, if that's how you were set up).
3. Click on the website. The menu on the left lists each part of the site.

## The golden rule
Make your changes, then click **Save**. The live site updates on its own in **1–2 minutes**. If you don't see the change, refresh the page in your browser.

---

## Add an animal
1. Click **Animals** in the menu.
2. Scroll to the list of animals and click the **Add** (+) button.
3. Fill in the name, upload a photo, choose the type, status, age, and sex, and write a few sentences about their personality.
4. Optional: paste a link to their adoption listing.
5. Tick **Show on the home page** if you want them featured. (The home page shows the first 3 that are ticked.)
6. Click **Save**.

**When an animal is adopted:** change their Status to **Adopted!** (a happy update for followers), or delete them from the list.

**To change the order:** move animals up or down in the list using the handle next to each entry.

## Add an event
1. Click **Events & News** and then the **Add** (+) button.
2. Fill in the name, date, time, location, and description. You can add a photo or flyer.
3. Click **Save**.

Events sort themselves by date. Once the date passes, an event automatically moves to "Past events." You don't need to delete it.

## Change the announcement bar (the strip at the top of every page)
1. Click **Site Settings & Contact Info**.
2. Scroll down to **Announcement bar**.
3. Type your message, e.g. "50/50 Raffle tickets on sale through Dec 31!", and add a link if you want.
4. Untick **Show the announcement bar** to hide it.
5. Click **Save**.

## Change words or photos on a page
Click the page name (Home Page, About Page, How to Help Page, Contact Page), edit the text or click a photo to replace it, and click **Save**.

## Update contact info, donate link, or social media
These are all in **Site Settings & Contact Info**. Anything left blank is simply hidden on the site.

---

## Photo tips
- Use **landscape or square** photos for animals. Close-ups of faces look best.
- Keep photos under about **2 MB**. Photos straight from a phone are fine, but if uploads are slow, email them to yourself and choose the "medium" size.
- Good light makes a huge difference: near a window or outside works best.

## Uh-oh, I made a mistake
Every save is backed up automatically. Contact the person who set up the site and they can restore any earlier version.
